I’m not good at saying this out loud, so I wrote it down where I can get it right.

You have changed my days. I used to check my phone for the time. Now I check it hoping to see a message from you.

I love your smile, and I still remember how cute you were the day I met you. I love how you talk about the things you care about, too. Your voice gets quicker and your hands begin to move, and while you talk, I find myself admiring you. I could listen for hours. I’ve tried.

I’m courting you because I want to do this properly. I want to earn your trust, meet the people who matter to you, and show you with my actions, not just my words, that I’m serious about you.

I’m not asking you to hurry. I only wanted you to know where I stand. I love who you are, and I’m very lucky to have you. I’m glad every day that you let me get to know you.
